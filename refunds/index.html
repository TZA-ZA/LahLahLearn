Refund Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

1. Summary

This Refund Policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for paid LahLahLearn plans. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

  • The Free plan costs nothing.
  • The 7-day free trial of the Family plan needs no payment details, so there is nothing to refund when it ends.
  • You can cancel a paid plan at any time.

2. Cancelling a plan

You may cancel at any time from the Plans page (“Manage plan”). Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you keep access to paid features until then. After that the account returns to the Free plan, and your child’s progress is kept.

Paid plans renew automatically at the end of each billing period until cancelled. To avoid the next charge, cancel before the renewal date shown on the Plans page.

3. Refunds

Except where required by law, payments are non-refundable and we do not give refunds for partial billing periods.

4. If you were charged in error

If you believe you were charged in error, for example you were charged twice or charged after cancelling, contact us at lahlahlearn@gmail.com within 14 days of the charge and we will review it promptly. Please include the email address of your account, the date of the charge and the amount.

If a refund is approved, it is returned through Stripe to the payment method you used. Your bank may take several working days to show it.

5. Price changes

We will give you at least 30 days’ notice before a new price applies to your subscription, and you may cancel before it takes effect.

6. Your legal rights

Nothing in this Policy affects your rights under the Consumer Protection Act 1999 of Malaysia or other laws that cannot be excluded.
