About LahLahLearn

LahLahLearn is a calm, playful learning app for children aged 3 to 8. Lessons are short, the screen stays quiet, and every child moves at their own pace.

Start free   See plans

What LahLahLearn is

LahLahLearn is a web app that runs in the browser on a computer, tablet or phone. A grown-up sets it up in a few minutes by answering gentle, everyday questions about the child. The app then adjusts its fonts, sounds, motion and lesson length to match.

Children learn reading, maths, feelings and everyday life skills with Pip, a friendly guide. Lessons take three to five minutes, show at most three choices at a time, and give only kind feedback. There are no failure screens and no red crosses.

Who it is for

LahLahLearn is made for children who learn best with short lessons, calm screens and a flexible pace. That includes children who learn differently, and children who are deaf or hard of hearing. It is used at home with a parent or guardian, and it can be shared with the teachers and therapists who support the child.

How it works

  1. A short set-up. A grown-up answers about 12 gentle questions. The child never takes a test.
  2. Learn in calm bursts. Three to five minutes at a time, with stars for effort, never scores.
  3. Share progress. The Care Circle shows parents, teachers and therapists the same goals and progress.

What is inside

Lessons in order

A step-by-step journey through classes. Each lesson opens the next, and a small test opens the next class.

Pip, a friendly guide

Pip reads aloud, cheers gently and can chat with the child in a safe, age-appropriate way.

Calm breaks

Breathing and quiet moments whenever a child needs one. Everything pauses until they are ready.

Stories and songs

Picture books that read aloud, sing-along songs, drawing, tracing letters and movement games.

A small 3D world

An island to walk around, with places to play and things to decorate with rewards from lessons.

Care Circle

One calm view of progress for the grown-ups, with weekly reports by email.

Plans

There is a Free plan, a paid Family plan with a 7-day free trial that needs no payment details, and a School or clinic plan. Prices are shown in your currency on the Plans page. You can cancel a paid plan at any time; see the Refund Policy.

Safety and privacy

  • Accounts are created by a parent or guardian aged 18 or over, who gives consent for the child.
  • Children are never asked for an email address, phone number or home address, and children never chat with each other.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • A parent can delete the account and all of the child’s data at any time in Settings. A code is emailed to the parent first, to check it is really them.

Good questions

Which children is LahLahLearn for?

Children who learn best with short lessons, calm screens and a flexible pace. That includes children who learn differently, and children who are deaf or hard of hearing.

Does my child take a test?

No. A grown-up answers a few questions at set-up, and the Lantern Quest is a three-minute story game. Your child never sees a score.

Why levels instead of grades?

Grades compare children with their classmates. Levels describe what a child can do today, so there is nothing to fall behind on.

Is it safe for my child to play with others?

Children never chat. The Hall of Fame is switched off until a grown-up turns it on, and it shows only a nickname and stars.

Does it replace therapy or school?

No. It adds practice between sessions, and the Care Circle shows teachers and therapists what happened so they can plan the next one.
