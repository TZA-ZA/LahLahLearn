Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how LahLahLearn (“LahLahLearn”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, stores and protects personal data when you use our website, web application and related services (together, the “Service”).

We process personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia (“PDPA”), as amended, and its Personal Data Protection Principles. Where users are located outside Malaysia, we also take into account the privacy laws that apply to them.

By creating an account or using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Service.

2. Who we are

LahLahLearn is a calm learning service for children aged 3 to 8, operated from Selangor, Malaysia. For the purposes of the PDPA, LahLahLearn is the data user responsible for the personal data described in this Policy. You can reach us at lahlahlearn@gmail.com.

3. Scope of this Policy

This Policy applies to:

  • Parents and guardians who create and manage family accounts;
  • Children whose learning profiles are created by a parent or guardian;
  • Care Circle members, such as teachers and therapists, invited by a parent; and
  • Visitors who browse our website without an account.

It does not apply to third-party websites or services that may be linked from the Service. Those services have their own privacy policies.

5. Information processed only on your device

Some optional features analyse data locally on the device. This data is not sent to us or stored by us.

  • Focus and feelings helper (camera). When a parent switches it on, camera images are analysed on the device to notice signs of tiredness, distraction or upset and to offer a calm break. Images and video are never recorded, stored or uploaded.
  • Speaking practice (microphone). When a child chooses to speak, speech recognition is performed by the web browser. Depending on the browser, its developer may process the audio under its own privacy policy.
  • Calm sounds and voice. Sounds and read-aloud speech are produced on the device.

Camera and microphone access is always requested through the browser’s own permission prompt, and can be withdrawn at any time in the browser settings.

7. The learning assistant (Pip)

When a child talks with Pip, the text of the conversation is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate a reply. We send only the message text needed for the reply, together with general settings such as the child’s first name, age and language. Pip is instructed not to ask children for personal information such as addresses, phone numbers or school names. Our AI provider does not use this data to train its models, and processes it under a data processing agreement.

8. Children’s privacy

The Service is designed for children under the supervision of a parent or guardian.

  • Accounts can only be created by an adult, who gives consent on the child’s behalf.
  • Children are never asked for an email address, phone number or home address.
  • Children’s names are only shown to others if a parent switches on the Hall of Fame.
  • There are no public profiles, chat with strangers or advertising.
  • A parent can view, change or delete a child’s information at any time.

If you believe a child’s information has been provided to us without a parent’s consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including:

  • encrypted (HTTPS) connections for all traffic;
  • database access rules that limit each user to their own family’s data;
  • hashed passwords and PINs, which we cannot read;
  • a PIN-protected parent area; and
  • limited staff access, granted only when needed for support.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to cause significant harm, we will notify affected users and the relevant authority as required by law, without undue delay.

14. Your rights

Subject to the PDPA and other applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you or your child;
  • Correct personal data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
  • Withdraw consent to processing, which may mean we can no longer provide the Service;
  • Delete your account and your child’s data;
  • Receive a copy of your data in a commonly used format (data portability); and
  • Limit processing, for example by switching off optional features.

Most of this can be done directly in the Care Circle and Settings. For anything else, email lahlahlearn@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we will respond within 21 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia.

15. Emails we send

We send service emails such as sign-in codes, receipts, weekly progress reports and Care Circle invitations. You can change the report day in the Care Circle, or ask us to stop weekly reports at any time. We do not send marketing emails without your consent, and every marketing email includes a way to unsubscribe.

16. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will tell users within the Service, and by email where appropriate, before the changes take effect. The effective date at the top shows when this Policy was last revised.
