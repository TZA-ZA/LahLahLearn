Cookie Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

1. What this Policy covers

This Cookie Policy explains how LahLahLearn uses cookies and similar browser storage (such as local storage) on our website and web application. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

2. What we use

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use browser storage as follows:

TypeWhat it doesCan I turn it off?
EssentialKeeps you signed in, saves learning progress and settings on the device, and remembers your cookie choiceNo. The Service cannot work without it
Optional statisticsAnonymous counts of which lessons are used, to help us improve them. No names or contact details are includedYes. It is off unless you choose “Accept all”

3. What we do not use

  • No advertising cookies and no advertising networks.
  • No cookies that follow you or your child across other websites.
  • No selling or sharing of browsing data.

4. Other companies

Some steps happen on pages run by other companies, which may set their own cookies there under their own policies:

  • Stripe, when you pay for a plan on Stripe’s checkout page; and
  • Google or Apple, if you choose to sign in with them.

5. Your choices

You can change your choice at any time through Cookie settings.

You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser settings. If you do, you will be signed out, and settings or progress saved only on that device may be removed.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last revised.
